# Fire pairs

Fire with Ice, Water, Lightning, Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow. Fire + Fire (Dragon) is under Pure fusions.

## Apocalypse — Fire + Ice

*The world ending both ways at once, and the shock where fire meets ice.*

- **Frostfire** (Debuff): counts as both an Ignite and Chilled (5 Affliction at the end of its applier's turn, and costs can't be reduced).

Once per unit per turn. Frostfire alone doesn't Shock, but anything of either kind applied after it does.

## Alchemy — Fire + Water

*Fire and water in the crucible: nothing is destroyed, only changed.*

- **Transmute**: turns effects on a unit into others, stack for stack, as the skill lists. The standard recipes: on enemies, Might becomes Weakness, Armor becomes Vulnerable, Focus becomes Confusion, and Renew becomes Weakness; on allies, the reverse, and other Debuffs become Renew.
- **Catalyst**: the next skill that affects the bearer is doubled for them: twice the stacks, amounts and durations. A Buff on allies, a Debuff on enemies.

## Plasma — Fire + Lightning

*Fire and lightning past their limits: power you ride until it melts down.*

- **Heat** (the user's, max 5): Plasma skills add the Heat they list (+1 or +2) after resolving, and the user also gains 1 Heat whenever they gain Charge. Plasma skills deal 5 more damage per Heat. At the end of a turn in which the user has 5 Heat, they **Melt Down**: they take 20 Affliction, every enemy takes 20 Affliction and is Sapped, and Heat returns to 0.
- **Vent**: a Vent skill removes all the user's Heat for the benefit it lists per Heat removed.

## Mechanic — Fire + Wind

*Fire in the boiler, wind in the bellows: machines you build, tune and send into the fight.*

- **Contraptions**: machine minions. They can't be healed or gain Renew (only Repair skills restore them), and they ignore Stun, Sleep and Confusion. Several skills build a **Turret** (20 HP), which deals 10 damage to a random enemy at the end of each of your turns.
- **Upgrade**: raises an allied minion one level (max 3). Each level gives +10 max HP (and 10 HP) and +5 damage on its skills.
