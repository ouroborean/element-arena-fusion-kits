# Holy & Unholy pairs

Holy with Unholy and Shadow; then Unholy with Shadow.

## Zealot — Holy + Unholy

*Holy and unholy in one soul: faith that feeds on suffering, and a death that serves the cause.*

- **Fervor** (Buff, max 5): Zealot skills grant it, and a unit that already has Fervor gains 1 more whenever an enemy damages them or gives them a Debuff. Each stack adds 5 damage and 5 healing to the bearer's Zealot skills.
- **Martyr**: when a unit with Fervor dies, each of their allies heals 10 per stack and gains 1 Might per 2 stacks.

## Curse — Unholy + Shadow

*Unholy in the shadows: hexes that punish every kind of choice, and outlive their victims.*

- **Hexes** (Debuffs), each punishing one kind of action for its duration:
    - **Hex of Pain**: whenever the bearer deals damage, they take 10 Affliction.
    - **Hex of Silence**: the bearer's Strategic skills cost 1 more random energy.
    - **Hex of Ruin**: whenever the bearer gains a Buff, they take 10 Affliction.
- **Lingering**: when a Hexed unit dies or has a Hex cleansed, that Hex jumps to a random ally of theirs with its remaining duration. Hexes that simply run out are gone.
