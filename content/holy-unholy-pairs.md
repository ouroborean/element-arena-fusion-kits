# Holy & Unholy pairs

Holy with Unholy and Shadow; then Unholy with Shadow.

## Zealot — Holy + Unholy

*Holy and unholy in one soul: faith that feeds on suffering, and a death that serves the cause.*

Plays like: Suffering fuels faith; even death helps

## Vigilante — Holy + Shadow

*Holy light from the shadows: justice outside the law, dragging hidden things into the open.*

Plays like: Works from the shadows to drag the enemy's into the light

## Curse — Unholy + Shadow

*Unholy in the shadows: hexes that punish every kind of choice, and outlive their victims.*

Plays like: Curses that punish choices and refuse to leave
