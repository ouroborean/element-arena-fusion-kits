# Ice pairs

Ice with Water, Lightning, Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow. Fire + Ice (Apocalypse) is under Fire pairs.

## Glacier — Ice + Water

*Ice that moves at its own pace: freeze the enemy's clock and let yours run.*

Plays like: Freeze their clock, speed up yours

A tempo kit: Ice's lockdown aimed at cooldowns, and Water's cooldown relief pushed further.

## Aurora — Ice + Lightning

*Ice and lightning lit up across the sky: the kit that bends energy colors.*

Plays like: The energy-color kit: free yourself, scramble them

Lightning changes how much energy a player has; Aurora changes which colors they have.

## Winter — Ice + Wind

*Ice on the wind: the season that pins everything in place.*

Plays like: Pins targets down, then uses Wind's tools against the Immobile

## Stasis — Ice + Poison

*Cold that stops the clock on poison: freeze the afflictions in place, then let them all go at once.*

Plays like: Freeze the poison, then release it all

## Myth — Ice + Earth

*Ice and stone that remember: the old legends of the frozen north, and the hero who becomes one.*

Plays like: A hero who becomes a giant of legend

## Prism — Ice + Holy

*Light through ice: split it to reach everyone, or focus it to burn through one.*

Plays like: Split light to spread it, focus it to burst

## Lich — Ice + Unholy

*Cold undeath: a sorcerer who hid their soul in a jar, and freezes the souls of others.*

Plays like: Break the jar first, or the Lich won't die

## Night — Ice + Shadow

*The cold that comes unseen, for enemies; chosen stillness, for allies.*

Plays like: The cold that comes unseen, on a clock only you can see

Dusk is hidden from the enemy, but the battle log still shows who used Evening Star on whom, so an attentive opponent can count the clocks.
