# Ice pairs

Ice with Water, Lightning, Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow. Fire + Ice (Apocalypse) is under Fire pairs.

## Glacier — Ice + Water

*Ice that moves at its own pace: freeze the enemy's clock and let yours run.*

- **Icebound** (Debuff): the bearer's cooldowns don't tick down while it lasts.
- **Meltwater** (Buff): the bearer's cooldowns tick down 1 extra at the end of each of their turns.

Glacier is a tempo kit: Ice's lockdown aimed at cooldowns, and Water's cooldown relief pushed further.

## Winter — Ice + Wind

*Ice on the wind: the season that pins everything in place.*

- **Snowbound** (Debuff; counts as a Frost debuff): the bearer counts as Immobile. They lose their mobility buffs (Swiftness, Rushing, Leaping), can't gain new ones, and their mobility skills (Charge, Maneuver, Mislead, Dance) cost 1 more.

One keyword: Wind already has tools that punish the Immobile, and Winter's own side stays fast (Rushing, Leaping) while the enemy's freezes.

## Stasis — Ice + Poison

*Cold that stops the clock on poison: freeze the afflictions in place, then let them all go at once.*

- **Suspended**: the bearer's effects don't tick, lose duration or deal damage while it lasts (Toxin, Ignite and Renew included). A Debuff on enemies; on allies, a Buff that makes their Buffs last longer. The bearer still acts normally.
- **Thaw**: when Suspended ends, all the bearer's Toxin deals its damage at once, doubled (10 Affliction per stack), and then keeps ticking as normal.
