# Lightning pairs

Lightning with Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow.

## Storm — Lightning + Wind

*Lightning on the wind: a storm your team keeps fed turn after turn, until it breaks over everyone.*

- **Tempest** (your team's, max 5, visible to both): +1 each time your team uses a Storm skill; −1 at the end of each of your turns in which none was used. Storm skills scale with it where they say so. At 5, the **Eye of the Storm**: your next Storm skill also hits every other enemy with its damage and effects, and Tempest drops to 3.

One keyword: a cheap Storm skill used every turn keeps the storm alive on its own, and Lightning and Wind supply the rest.
