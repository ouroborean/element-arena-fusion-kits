# Lightning pairs

Lightning with Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow.

## Storm — Lightning + Wind

*Lightning on the wind: a storm your team keeps fed turn after turn, until it breaks over everyone.*

Plays like: Feed the storm every turn, or it dies down

## Battery — Lightning + Poison

*Stored lightning in a leaking cell: charge up slowly, let it all out at once, and let the acid eat through defenses.*

Plays like: Charge up slowly while eating through defenses

## Magnet — Lightning + Earth

*Lightning in iron and stone: pull what the enemy relies on toward you, and push your problems onto them.*

Plays like: Steals defenses and pushes problems back

## Vengeance — Lightning + Holy

*Holy lightning that answers every blow: hurt us, and the strike comes back harder.*

Plays like: Hurt us, and we hit back harder

## Reanimation — Lightning + Unholy

*Lightning in dead flesh: death is a setback, not an ending.*

Plays like: Revival with a jolt, and a shelf life

## Ion — Lightning + Shadow

*Lightning in the dark: a pulse that switches the enemy off.*

Plays like: An EMP for buffs and minions
