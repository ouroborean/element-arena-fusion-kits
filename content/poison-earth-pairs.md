# Poison & Earth pairs

Poison with Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow; then Earth with Holy, Unholy and Shadow.

## Spore — Poison + Earth

*Poison in the soil: an infection that spreads from host to host and fruits into a colony.*

Plays like: Infect one, and the colony spreads

## Antidote — Poison + Holy

*Poison made medicine by holy hands: protection that learns, and cleansing that strikes back.*

Plays like: Protection that learns; cleansing that hits back

## Blight — Poison + Unholy

*Poison and death: a rot that takes what never grows back.*

Plays like: Rot: permanent attrition that only cleansing answers

## Assassin — Poison + Shadow

*Poison from the dark: a contract only you can see, and a blade that ends it.*

Plays like: A hidden contract: they never know who's marked

Death Mark is hidden from the enemy: the skill in playing against it is working out who is marked.

## Sanctuary — Earth + Holy

*Holy ground: stone raised into a fortress that shelters the whole team.*

Plays like: Holy ground: a fortress for the whole team

## Grave — Earth + Unholy

*Earth that keeps the dead: every fallen body, on either side, becomes another soldier.*

Plays like: Every death, on either side, feeds the army

## Moon — Earth + Shadow

*Stone that shines in the dark: a four-beat rhythm of hiding, growing, striking and draining.*

Plays like: A four-beat rhythm: hide, grow, strike, drain

The cycle is predictable, so both players can plan around the Full Moon.
