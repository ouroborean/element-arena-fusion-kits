# Poison & Earth pairs

Poison with Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow; then Earth with Holy, Unholy and Shadow.

## Spore — Poison + Earth

*Poison in the soil: an infection that spreads from host to host and fruits into a colony.*

- **Spores** (Debuff, stacks): at the end of each turn, a bearer with 2 or more Spores passes 1 to a random ally of theirs. When a unit reaches 3, a **Mushroom** sprouts from them for the Spore player and they lose the 3 Spores. A Mushroom is a 15 HP minion that counts as a Seedling; at the end of your turns it Puffs, giving a random enemy 1 Spore.

One keyword: the colony it grows plugs straight into Earth's Seedling skills.

## Assassin — Poison + Shadow

*Poison from the dark: a contract only you can see, and a blade that ends it.*

- **Death Mark** (Debuff, hidden): the enemy can't see it. Assassin skills deal 10 more to the bearer, and any Assassin skill that leaves them at 25 HP or less executes them. It's revealed when it triggers or expires. Each Assassin has one Death Mark out at a time. Some skills raise its execute threshold (never above 40 HP) or move it to another enemy; a moved Mark stays hidden.

One keyword: like Night's clocks, the skill in playing against it is working out who is marked.
