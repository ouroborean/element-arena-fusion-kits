# Pure fusions

One element taken twice: Fire + Fire is Dragon, Ice + Ice is Crystal, and so on.

## Dragon — Fire + Fire

*Fire at its peak: a wyrm whose burns fill its hoard, whose hoard hardens its scales, and who spends it all on one breath.*

Plays like: Grows hotter and harder, then spends it all on one breath

## Crystal — Ice + Ice

*Ice made perfect: flawless for allies, fatally flawed for enemies.*

Plays like: Shatter combos on offense, burst-proof allies on defense

## Ocean — Water + Water

*Water without limits: every skill rises and falls like a wave.*

Plays like: A rhythm: plan which half of each skill lands when

## Thunder — Lightning + Lightning

*The sound after the flash: every strike lands twice, and the roar drowns out the enemy's tricks.*

Plays like: Hits twice, and switches off the enemy's tricks

## Cloud — Wind + Wind

*Wind that gathers instead of rushing: slow, telegraphed, and heavy when it breaks.*

Plays like: Wind's speed traded for weight: slow, telegraphed, heavy

## Evolution — Poison + Poison

*Poison that adapts: every use evolves the skill.*

Plays like: Weak openers that become the team's strongest skills

## Life — Earth + Earth

*Earth that grows: a garden that becomes a grove, and a team that keeps getting bigger.*

Plays like: A garden, and a team that keeps getting bigger

## Divine — Holy + Holy

*Holy made absolute: one light that heals whoever it touches on your side and burns whoever it touches on theirs.*

Plays like: Always useful: heal or smite as the turn needs

## Evil — Unholy + Unholy

*Unholy with nothing held back: it poisons healing itself, and spends souls like coin.*

Plays like: Punishes healers, feeds on souls

## Dimension — Shadow + Shadow

*Shadow folded into space: pull a unit out of the fight, or tie two together so one effect lands twice.*

Plays like: Takes a unit out of play; makes one effect count twice
