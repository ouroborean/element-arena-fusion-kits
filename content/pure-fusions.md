# Pure fusions

One element taken twice. These are the easiest fusions to reach: a character's locked native infusion plus one more of the same element.

*Fire at its peak: a wyrm whose burns fill its hoard, whose hoard hardens its scales, and who spends it all on one breath.*

## Cloud — Wind + Wind

*Wind that gathers instead of rushing: slow, telegraphed, and heavy when it breaks.*

- **Drift**: a Drift skill hangs in plain sight and lands at the start of the user's next turn, on the same targets if they're still valid (otherwise on another valid one). - **Aloft** (Buff): the bearer counts as Leaping (+5 direct damage, and Wind's Leaping payoffs) for its whole duration; dealing damage doesn't end it. Unlike a Leap, it gives no Invulnerability.
