# Water pairs

Water with Lightning, Wind, Poison, Earth, Holy, Unholy and Shadow. Water's pairs with Fire and Ice are under those tabs.

## Current — Water + Lightning

*Water carries lightning: soak the enemy team, and one strike runs through all of them.*

- **Soaked** (Debuff): the bearer takes 5 more damage from Current skills. When a single-target Current skill damages a Soaked unit, every other Soaked unit on that side takes the same damage. Skills that already hit everyone don't conduct.

One keyword: the rest is Water's Renew and Flow, and Lightning's Charge and Sapped.

## Serum — Water + Poison

*Water and poison in one syringe: the dose makes the medicine, and the dose makes the poison.*

- **Dose** (a Buff, on any unit): at the end of its applier's turn, the bearer heals 5 per stack. A unit that reaches 4 Dose **Overdoses**: they take 10 Affliction per stack and lose all Dose. Allies are kept below 4; enemies are pushed past it. As a Buff, Immune doesn't stop it, but Horrified does.

One keyword: every Serum skill is a question of how many Doses, and on whom.

## Slime — Water + Earth

*Water and earth become ooze: it splits when struck, swallows what it touches, and keeps coming back.*

- **Oozes**: minions that **Split**. When an Ooze takes damage and survives with 10 or more HP, a new Ooze appears and the two share its remaining HP. Max 4 Oozes per side. Skills that create an Ooze make one with 20 HP and Slap (nc): 5 damage.
- **Engulf** (Debuff): the bearer is inside an Ooze. They're Stunned and take 5 Affliction at the end of each of its owner's turns, until that Ooze dies or 3 turns pass. Their allies can free them by killing the Ooze.

## Mirror — Water + Shadow

*Still water in the dark: whatever the enemy does comes back at them, or is copied.*

- **Reflect**: Mirror skills set up the game's existing Reflect (a counter that applies the skill to its user, or their team) against the next skill of a given kind: Harmful skills aimed at your side, or even the enemy's Helpful skills, whose effects then land on your side instead. Water's Flow still ignores it.
- **Mimic**: the user uses a copy of the last skill a given enemy used, as its user, choosing any targets it needs. It costs nothing extra.
